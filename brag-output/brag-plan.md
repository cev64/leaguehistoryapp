# /brag plan: Pigskin Pantheon

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 24s · **Tone:** `default` (punchy, playful, clean) with a little broadcast swagger

## The answers

- **What is it?** A hall of fame for your Sleeper or ESPN fantasy football league: every season, recap, record and grudge on one site.
- **Who's it for?** Fantasy football leagues with history and a group chat full of arguments.
- **What sets it apart?** It remembers everything Sleeper and ESPN forget, and it has an AI historian that "talks trash with receipts."
- **Most impressive / funniest claim:** The League Historian: "GridironGreg owns DraftDayDana. It's 8–0 all-time. Somebody check that lineup for a curse." The record book backs it up: 8–0, +230.76.
- **Visual hook:** A real Ask the League chat typing out a roast.
- **Real UI to show:** The demo league (Sunday Scaries) running in the actual app: Ask the League, the all-time manager card's head-to-head table, the weekly recap, and the 3D trophy room.
- **Share caption:** Your league has stories. Pigskin Pantheon gives them a home.

## Angle

**Trash talk, with receipts.** Open on the AI roast, reveal the brand, then show the receipts: the record book proving the 8–0, the recap the group chat reads, and the trophy room where the winners live forever.
