# /brag plan: Pigskin Pantheon

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 25s · **Tone:** `default` (punchy, clean), with a hall-of-fame sense of occasion

## The answers

- **What is it?** A hall of fame for your Sleeper or ESPN fantasy football league: every season, champion, manager and player on one site.
- **Who's it for?** Fantasy football leagues that have been playing together for years.
- **What sets it apart?** It remembers everything Sleeper and ESPN don't: a walk-through 3D trophy room, a locker for every manager, a history for every player who touched a roster, and an AI historian that knows the whole league.
- **Most impressive claim:** You can walk your league's hall of fame in 3D, and the league has its own historian.
- **Visual hook:** Gliding down the Cup Room past real championship trophies.
- **Real UI to show:** The demo league (Sunday Scaries) in the actual app: the 3D hall (Cup Room, Hall of Fame, Record Wall), a team locker, a player card, and Ask the League.
- **Share caption:** Your league has stories. Pigskin Pantheon gives them a home.

## Angle

**Your league's history, finally a place you can walk through.** Open inside the trophy room, reveal the brand, then tour the hall, step into a manager's locker, look up a player's life in the league, and ask the league's own historian a real question.

## Visual identity

From `landing.css` / `design.css`: navy `#071827`, gold `#f6b73c`, red `#d71920`. Barlow Condensed 700 uppercase for display, Inter for body. The crest icon (`icons/crest-master.png`).

## Notes
