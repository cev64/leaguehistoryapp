Find your leagues

Sleeper's league data is public, so your username is all it takes: no password, nothing to connect. Or paste a league ID.

Your league ID is the number after leagueId= in your league's address on fantasy.espn.com; pasting the whole link works too. A league that's viewable to the public needs nothing else.

Opening a private ESPN league

Connect it from a computer, once. A private league opens with two keys ESPN keeps in your browser, and they can only be copied from a desktop browser's developer tools, which phones and tablets don't have.

  1. On a computer, open this site and sign in.
  2. Pick ESPN ▸ Private league? and save your keys to your account.
  3. Back here, your private leagues are listed and open with nothing to type.

Have your keys already? Enter them here

A private league opens with your ESPN keys: two values ESPN keeps in your browser once you're signed in. Find them on a computer:

  1. Sign in at fantasy.espn.com and open your league.
  2. Open the browser's developer tools (F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac) ▸ Application (Chrome, Edge) or Storage (Firefox, Safari) ▸ Cookies ▸ espn.com.
  3. Copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID into the boxes below.
