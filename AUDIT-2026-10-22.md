# Security, bug and UX audit — 2026-10-22

Eight reviews of the web site, the Supabase backend and the iOS app, all read-only except the one fix noted.
Most severe first in each section.

✅ **checked**: I opened the code myself and confirmed it. Everything else: a subagent traced it in the code but nobody ran it.

## Fixed already

- ✅ **Stored XSS through a division name**
  - `season.html:6421` (`seedTag`) put `label.note` (`"<division name> leader"`) into a `title` attribute without escaping.
  - A commissioner who named a division `x" onmouseover=…` would run script for everyone who opened the season page.
  - That script could read ESPN keys and the Supabase refresh token from localStorage.
  - Now `esc(label.note)`. Not in the iOS engine, which has no `seedTag`.
- **Ask the League runs only on Claude Haiku 5.5.** Gemini is gone, so there's no paid fallback left to trigger. The function was deployed 2026-10-22.
- **iOS notification switches** now say "This device can't get notifications yet." when there's no push token.
- **"New chat" during an answer** no longer corrupts the conversation, and saved chats heal themselves.
- **Record book PF/G** is over head-to-head games in median leagues, on the web and in the app.
- **ESPN keys are cleared on sign-out** on the web and on iOS. iOS Home's Sign out now asks first.
- **The web asks for consent** before the first Ask the League question.

Everything below is still open unless it's one of the items above.

## Confusing for users

### Both platforms
- **Errors are raw and have no retry.** Users see "Sleeper answered 503", "Load failed", "The relay answered 502" and "The league AI answered 500."
  - Web: `season.html:4657`, `alltime.html:1800`, `moves.html:1777`, `trophy/app.js:158`.
  - iOS: `EmptyCard` (`Design/Components.swift:271`), which has no action slot.
  - Map errors to plain words in one place and add **Try again**.
- **ESPN jargon.** "espn_s2 and SWID", "relay", "keys", "ESPN id …1A2B3", and "Forget" with no confirmation. Use "your ESPN sign-in" and "Disconnect", with a confirmation step. On iOS, the "Private league?" row hides the best path; rename it "Sign in to find your leagues".
- **"Sync / Unsync" is internal vocabulary.** On the web, opening any shared link silently adds that league to your account. Use "Added", "Remove from account", and a toast on auto-add.
- **Ask the League has four names:** Ask the League, League Historian, AI historian, the league AI. Pick one.
- **"Rivalry & stakes"** (iOS `WeekGamesCard.swift:524`, also visible in the landing screenshot) is betting language. Use "Rivalry preview", which is already the VoiceOver label.

### Web
- **Pick'em and awards are hard to find.** A live season opens on last week's Results. Add a hero banner: "Week 8 picks lock Thu 8:15 PM · Make your picks →".
- **The lock rule is unexplained.** All picks lock together at the first kickoff, and only one Lock is allowed, but users learn both only from errors.
- **A week in progress says "Not yet played"** from Thursday to Tuesday (`season.html:6682`).
- **The "Season" dropdown also holds All-Time and Front Office.** Front Office has no other entry point, and the logo goes to the record book, not home.
- **The confirmation email lands on the front page,** not the shared league link the person came from (`account.js:188, :198`).
- **ESPN league pages lose the Privacy, Terms and Support footer** (`sleeper.js:1544` replaces the whole footer).
- **Account deletion is missing on the web.** It's iOS only; at least say "email support".
- **Old `?invite=` links still show "Pro … unlimited leagues and no ads"** (`account.js:1617`, not gated by `PRICING`).
- **Leaderboard columns** "Perf", "Won" and "Streak" explain themselves only in tooltips, which don't work on phones. The tiebreaker "i" buttons are 17 px tap targets.

### iOS
- **Account copy says an account is required ("free with an account: every season… the trophy room") when it isn't.** Use "Create account", not "Create free account". The dormant `free_limit` copy in `AccountStore.swift:458` is still plan language.
- **Sign out on Home has no confirmation;** Account does.
- **"Sign in" means three things** on the finder: the app account, ESPN, and saving to the account.
- **The ESPN sign-in sheet gives no feedback** when the cookies never arrive.
- **The phone tab bar is icon-only;** the briefcase for Front Office is the least guessable.
- **Dynamic Type:** 326 fixed `.font(.system(size:))` calls, some as small as 9.5pt on key chips.
- **Share this league** on a private ESPN league sends a link the recipient can't open.
- **Report Answer is long-press only.** App Store guideline 1.2 expects it to be findable.
