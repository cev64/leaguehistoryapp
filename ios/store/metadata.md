## Description (max 4000 characters): 3118/4000

Plain text. Paste as is.

```
Your league has stories. Give them a home.

Pigskin Pantheon turns the whole history of your fantasy football league into one place: every season, every score, every trade, every grudge. Open your league, scroll back to year one and settle the argument for good.

Free. Works with Sleeper and ESPN leagues. Made for iPhone and iPad.

TRY IT FIRST
Tap "Explore the demo league" to look around Sunday Scaries, a made-up ten-team dynasty league with five seasons of drafts, trades, waivers and playoffs. No league and no account needed.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED TO OPEN A LEAGUE
Type a Sleeper username, or paste an ESPN league ID or link, and pick a league. Every season it has played comes in at once. Sleeper needs only your username. A public ESPN league needs only its ID. Pigskin Pantheon never asks for your Sleeper or ESPN password.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Every season: week-by-week results, box scores and standings as they stood each week
- The playoff picture: seeds, clinches and brackets, projected or as played, toilet bowls included
- An all-time record book: titles, last places, playoff records and head-to-head against every manager
- Player cards: who rostered him, his best games and his record as a starter
- A walk-through 3D trophy room: the cup room, hall of fame, lowlight wall and the cellar
- The front office: trades judged in hindsight, waiver pickups and FAAB, lineup efficiency and where every draft pick ended up
- Weekly recaps with a headline and a story for every game, power rankings, and share cards for the group chat

Redraft, keeper or dynasty, with or without divisions or medians. The history goes back as far as each platform keeps it, so some older ESPN seasons are missing lineups.

ASK THE LEAGUE
Ask the League is an AI historian that answers from your league's own data: scores, standings, trades, waiver claims, drafts and lineups. Who owns who? Who lost the worst trade ever? You get the numbers, with a little smack when the numbers earn it. The smack stays on fantasy results.

Ask the League needs a free account. The first time you ask, the app tells you what would be sent and asks your permission before anything is shared with its third-party AI providers, Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude. You can turn that off again from the chat menu. Answers are labelled as AI, they can be wrong, and the record book has the final word. Long-press an answer to report it. A daily limit on questions applies.

YOUR ACCOUNT (OPTIONAL, FREE)
An account keeps your leagues on every device, saves your ESPN keys and unlocks Ask the League. Skip it and every league still opens. You can delete your account, and everything saved to it, from inside the app.

PRIVATE ESPN LEAGUES
A private ESPN league opens with two keys ESPN stores in your web browser. The app shows you where to find them, and you copy them from a computer once. If you save them to your account, they are stored encrypted.

Pigskin Pantheon is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper, ESPN, the NFL or the NFLPA.

Privacy Policy: https://pigskinpantheon.com/privacy.html · Terms: https://pigskinpantheon.com/terms.html
```

Everything else: Not collected. That includes Purchases, Financial Info, Health and Fitness, Location, Sensitive Info, Contacts, Browsing History, Search History, Device ID, Advertising Data, Usage Data, Diagnostics (no crash or analytics SDK) and Photos or Videos (saving a recap picture to Photos only writes to the library; nothing is read or sent).

Notes for the form:

- All five data types are only collected when someone makes an account or uses Ask the League. Opening a league or the demo without an account collects nothing from the person. Apple's label still has to list them, because the app can collect them.
- League data is read from Sleeper's and ESPN's public or keyed APIs directly on the device. It is not sent to Pigskin Pantheon's servers except as the league text in a question to Ask the League.
- Data sent to Google and Anthropic counts as collected through third-party partners. Their handling is covered in the privacy policy.
- Export compliance: `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` is in Info.plist (standard HTTPS only), so the encryption question is answered for you.
