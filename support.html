FAQ

How do I open my league?

Pick your platform on the front page. The app needs no account; the website asks you to sign in.

  • Sleeper: enter your Sleeper username and pick a league.
  • ESPN: enter the league ID or paste its link. A public league opens from that; a private one needs ESPN sign-in (below).
  • Open the demo league to look around.
How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose “Forgot password” and follow the emailed link. No email? Check spam and the address you signed up with.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account, then Delete Account. It removes your profile, synced leagues, any ESPN keys saved to your account and your chat usage counts. Or email support@pigskinpantheon.com from your account's address and we'll delete it. Data cached on your device goes when you delete the app. Details: Privacy Policy.

How do I open a private ESPN league?

Sign in to ESPN once. Pigskin Pantheon keeps the two keys ESPN gives your browser (espn_s2 and SWID), never your password.

  • iPhone or iPad app: ESPN tab › “Private league?” › Sign in with ESPN. ESPN's own page opens; sign in and your leagues appear.
  • Computer: add the Pigskin Pantheon Connect extension (Chrome, Edge or Firefox), then on the website's ESPN tab click Connect ESPN. The extension reads the two keys only when you click and stores nothing.

Phone browsers can't connect ESPN, but keys saved to your account work there too. If ESPN signs you out, connect again. Forget removes the keys. Or make the league public in ESPN's settings and the ID is enough.

Why is some of my league's history missing?

We show what Sleeper and ESPN provide. ESPN seasons before about 2018 have scores and standings but no lineups, and its trades before 2019 are patchy. A Sleeper league that was recreated rather than renewed won't link to its older seasons.

How does Ask the League work?

It's an AI that answers questions about your league. Your question and a text summary of the league go to Anthropic Claude, and the app and the site ask first. It can be wrong, so check anything important. Accounts get a daily question limit. See the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Is it free?

Yes. The app has no in-app purchases and no ads.

Is Pigskin Pantheon affiliated with Sleeper, ESPN, the NFL or the NFLPA?

No. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper, ESPN, the NFL or the NFLPA. League data comes from Sleeper's and ESPN's own services.

Something is broken. What should I send?

What you did, what happened, the league (Sleeper username or ESPN league ID; never your password or ESPN keys), your device and app version or browser. A screenshot helps.
